Channel Studio

Channel Studio (personal uploader) is the private command-line tool Brightvane Studio uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channels and to see how they're doing. It uses YouTube API Services. It isn't offered to the public and has no other users.

  • It uploads a video only after the channel owner reviews the exact title, description, tags, audience setting, visibility and destination channel, and confirms that one video, on the computer or by tapping on their phone.
  • It reads the owner's own channel and video statistics, shows them on screen, and never saves them.
  • With the owner's approval, it can post the same Short as a Reel to the owner's Instagram account through Meta's official API.
  • It never likes, comments, subscribes or watches on anyone's behalf, and never downloads YouTube videos.
  • It's operated with an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude), which makes the videos and may use the saved sign-in for read-only checks, such as statistics. The assistant can't upload: every upload is confirmed by the channel owner in person. When the owner asks in a live chat, it may edit the owner's channel profile and settings in the owner's own browser, where the owner can watch.
