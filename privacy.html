Channel Studio (personal uploader)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Channel Studio is a personal tool that its developer uses to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels and to see how those videos are doing. It is not offered to the public, and it has no other users.

YouTube API Services

Channel Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Channel Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What information is accessed, collected and stored

When you sign in with Google, you grant two permissions: upload videos to YouTube (youtube.upload) and view your YouTube account (youtube.readonly). With them, Channel Studio:

DataWhyStored?
OAuth access/refresh tokenTo act for you after you sign inYes, in token.json on your computer, until you revoke access
Your channel's name and IDTo show which channel an upload goes to before you confirmNo, read when needed
ID, upload time and visibility of videos uploaded through Channel StudioTo avoid uploading the same video twice, and to link to itYes, on your computer. Re-checked with YouTube at least every 30 days, and removed if the video no longer exists
Your channel's list of uploads, with public view, like and comment countsShown to you, or read by your AI assistant (below), so you can see what worksNo. Used during that run only, never saved

Channel Studio does not collect your Google password, contacts, email content, payment details, or any data about your viewers. It never downloads YouTube videos.

How information is used, processed and shared

  • Everything is stored only on your own computer. Channel Studio has no server and no database, and it sends nothing to anyone except Google/YouTube (over HTTPS) to carry out the actions you approve.
  • AI assistant. Channel Studio is operated together with an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude), which runs on the developer's computer as the developer's agent. It creates the videos and their titles, descriptions and tags. It may also use Channel Studio's saved sign-in on the developer's behalf for read-only actions: listing the channel's videos, reading their view, like and comment counts, and re-checking that stored records are still current. Information the assistant reads this way is processed by Anthropic to provide the assistant. Anthropic's privacy policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • What the AI assistant cannot do: it can't sign in or grant access (only the developer can, in person), and it can't upload, change or delete anything on YouTube. Every upload is started and confirmed by the developer, video by video.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used for any other purpose.
  • Every upload happens only after you review the exact title, description, tags, audience setting, visibility and destination channel, and confirm that one video (typed on the computer, or tapped on a private approval page that only works on the developer's own home network with a secret link).

Cookies, device storage, ads and third-party content

Channel Studio is a command-line program. It uses no cookies, no tracking, no analytics and no ads, and shows no third-party content. Its only device storage is the local files described above. This website itself sets no cookies.

Revoking access and deleting your data

  • Revoke access at any time in your Google Account's security settings: myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Or run python youtube_api.py --revoke [channel folder]. This revokes the token with Google immediately and deletes all YouTube data Channel Studio stored for that channel.
  • If access is revoked in Google's settings, or a login can't be refreshed, Channel Studio deletes the stored data for that channel the next time it runs. Either way, deletion happens within 7 days.

Deleting this local data does not delete or change anything on YouTube. Your videos stay on your channel.
