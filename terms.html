Channel Studio (personal uploader)

Terms of Use

Last updated: 14 October 2026

  1. What it is. Channel Studio is a personal command-line tool that its developer uses to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels and to view those channels' statistics. It is not offered to the public.
  2. YouTube Terms. Channel Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Channel Studio, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Use of your data is described in the Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
  3. You stay in control. Nothing is published without your specific approval. Before each upload you see the destination channel, title, description, tags, audience (made for kids or not), AI-content disclosure and visibility, and you must type a confirmation. You can change any of them before approving.
  4. Your content. You're responsible for what you upload. It must follow YouTube's Terms, Community Guidelines and copyright rules. Channel Studio never uploads on its own schedule, and never likes, comments, subscribes or views on anyone's behalf.
  5. Revoking access. You can revoke Channel Studio's access at any time (myaccount.google.com/connections, or python youtube_api.py --revoke). Its stored data is then deleted.
